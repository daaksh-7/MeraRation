Frequently Asked Questions

What is ration card portability?

It lets a card holder collect their entitled grains from any fair price shop in the country by biometric/Aadhaar-based authentication, useful for migrant workers and families.

Who is eligible for a ration card?

Eligibility is set by each state/UT based on income and household criteria. Priority Household (PHH) and Antyodaya (AAY) are the main categories. (Demo content.)

What documents do I need to apply?

Typically an identity proof, address proof, passport size photos and, where applicable, an income certificate.

How do I add a newborn or new spouse to my card?

Login → Dashboard → “Add / remove member”. Upload a birth/marriage certificate for verification.

My shop is not giving ration. What do I do?

Lodge a grievance on this portal or call the toll-free helpline 1967 / 14445.
